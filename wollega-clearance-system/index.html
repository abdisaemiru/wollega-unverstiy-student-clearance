How the clearance process works

Four steps between you and your finished clearance certificate.

01

Log in & submit

Sign in with your student ID and submit a clearance request with your program details.

02

Offices review

Library, Finance, Registrar and other offices review your standing and respond independently.

03

Track progress

Watch your clearance percentage rise and resolve any rejections directly with the office involved.

04

Get cleared

Once every office approves, download and print your official clearance certificate.
